# Quotie VSL — “We build the quoting. You send it.”

**Where it lives:** `/apply` after opt-in. Same video Benji tells them to watch before Locky.

**Who talks:** Locky. To camera. Like a chat, not a keynote.

**Length:** ~22–28 minutes. Don’t race it. Water on the desk is fine.

**Job of this video**

1. Make them nod: quoting is sitting on you, and it’s costing jobs and nights.
2. Be clear what Quotie is, and walk how a quote actually runs.
3. Kill “software you set up yourself.”
4. Shut Excel / hire / “I’ll get my guy to do it” before the call.
5. Handle the worries in their head so the apply isn’t a maybe.
6. Get the person who can say yes to hit Apply and book.

**Don’t say**

- The $5,800 / $7,800 numbers (ads hide price)
- “Set up in minutes”
- “Build your own templates”
- Invoices, taking payments, texts
- Fake names. No Virtue Roofing.
- Email open tracking (we track the quote page, not the email)
- More than four options on a quote
- AI that writes quotes

**Do say**

- We build it. You fill it in and send.
- Most businesses are sending real quotes in the first week.
- If you can’t invest in getting it built, don’t apply.
- Bring a quote you’ve actually sent.

**Spoken pace:** slow. Pause after numbers. Pause after “we build it for you.”

**Look:** you, plain background or the office. Cut in a real quote PDF, the form, a signed page if you have one. No stock handshakes.

**On-screen text:** short lines only. They’re in the script in `[CAPS]`.

**CTA on the page:** Apply Now · See if we should build it for you · 2 minutes.

CTA cards in the video at ~8:00, ~16:00, and the end. People drop off. Catch them.

---

## How to split-test the hook

Film each hook as its own first 30–45 seconds. Same shirt, same seat, same light.

Every hook ends on the same line: **“I’m Locky.”**

Then splice the same body from **§1b** onward. Don’t re-film the 25 minutes.

Run 2–3 at a time on `/apply`. Winner = apply rate, then booked calls, then cash. Not watch time.

Start with **A vs B vs D**. Those are the three different ideas. C, E, F are extras if a winner is close.

**G–L** call out the process. Use these if people are nodding at the ad but bouncing on the video — they need to hear their week back. Run **G vs A** once you have a control.

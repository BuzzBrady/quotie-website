Today we're officially launching Quotie — quoting software built specifically for trades businesses in Australia and New Zealand.

This is a milestone we've been working toward for a while, and we wanted to share a bit about what we've built, why we built it, and what's coming next.

## Why We Built Quotie

The quoting problem in trades is specific. It's not just that existing software is too expensive or too complex (though both are often true). It's that most quoting tools are built for a different kind of business — one with standard products, predictable pricing, and salespeople who have time to manually build proposals.

Trades businesses are different. Pricing is conditional. A solar job depends on roof pitch, shading, phase, and whether the customer wants a battery. An electrical quote depends on the age of the switchboard, the number of circuits, and what the customer is actually trying to accomplish. An HVAC job depends on room size, existing ductwork, and preferred brand.

This conditional complexity makes most generic quoting tools useless. You end up back in spreadsheets, which have the flexibility but none of the polish, speed, or tracking.

Quotie is built around this reality. The quoting engine handles conditional logic natively — show this option only if that answer was selected, calculate this price based on that measurement, require this field if that option is chosen. You build your pricing rules once, and the system handles the rest.

## What We've Built

**Smart quote templates.** Build your pricing system once with conditional logic, automatic calculations, and matrix pricing. Generate complete, accurate quotes in under 30 seconds.

**Multi-option proposals.** Send clients a single branded proposal with 2–8 pricing options laid out side by side. The most effective way to increase both close rate and average job value.

**Pipeline visibility.** See every open quote, when it was sent, whether it's been viewed, and when it's due for follow-up. Your entire sales pipeline at a glance.

**Automated follow-ups.** Set follow-up schedules and let the system remind you (or email clients automatically) when quotes go stale. Stop letting hot leads go cold.

**Branded email sending.** Send quotes from your own email address via Gmail or Outlook. Clients receive a professional email from you, not from a third-party platform. Track opens and replies natively.

**Calendar integration.** Accepted quotes flow straight into your calendar as jobs. No double-entry, no missed bookings.

**CRM basics.** Client profiles, quote history, contact details, and communication logs — everything you need to manage your customer relationships without a full CRM subscription.

**Integrations.** Native connections to Go High Level, ServiceM8, and Google Drive. More coming.

Today we're officially launching Quotie. Quoting software built specifically for trades businesses in Australia and New Zealand.

We wanted to share what we've built, why, and what's next.

## Why We Built It

The quoting problem in trades is specific. Most quoting tools are built for businesses with standard products and predictable pricing. Trades are different.

A solar job depends on roof pitch, shading, phase, and whether the customer wants a battery. An electrical quote depends on switchboard age, circuit count, and what the customer is trying to accomplish. A roofing job depends on profile type, access requirements, and whether there's demolition involved.

This conditional complexity makes generic quoting tools useless. You end up back in spreadsheets, which have the flexibility but none of the speed, polish, or tracking.

Quotie handles conditional logic natively. Show this option only if that answer was selected. Calculate this price based on that measurement. Require this field when that option is chosen. Build your rules once, the system handles the rest.

## What's In the Box

- **Smart quote templates.** Conditional logic, automatic calculations, matrix pricing. Complete quotes in under 30 seconds.
- **Multi-option proposals.** 2-8 pricing options in a single branded proposal, side by side.
- **Pipeline visibility.** Every open quote, when it was sent, when follow-up is due.
- **Follow-up dashboard.** Outstanding quotes sorted by urgency. Reschedule with one click.
- **Email sending.** Send from your own Gmail or Outlook. Replies tracked in Quotie automatically.
- **Calendar.** Day, week, month views with drag-and-drop rescheduling.
- **CRM basics.** Client profiles, quote history, contact details, communication logs.
- **Integrations.** GoHighLevel, ServiceM8, Google Drive. More coming.

## Who It's For

Quotie works for any trade business that sends quotes. We've built deepest support for solar, electrical, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, and painting.

If your quoting process currently involves spreadsheets, Word docs, or a generic invoicing tool, this was built for you.

## What's Coming

We're not slowing down:

- **Customer quote portal.** Clients review options, ask questions, and accept from a branded link.
- **Site visits.** Schedule and track directly from Quotie, linked to quotes and contacts.
- **More integrations** based on what our customers actually use.
- **Mobile app.** Works great in mobile browser today, dedicated app is coming.

## How We're Building

Every feature in the platform came from conversations with real trades businesses about what they actually need. If you're using Quotie and have feedback, we want to hear it. The fastest way to shape what we build next is to tell us.

Thanks for being here at the start. We're just getting going.
